UTAGE ENGLISH v0.1 BETA — KNOWN ISSUES
Updated: 10 October 2026

THIS IS A REAL BETA, NOT A 100% COMPLETE RELEASE.

1. FMV VIDEO: The large reauthored movies are NOT in this CORE package. Original Japanese PAM movies are copied from the player's own disc. English in-engine text and subtitles remain included.
2. CHARACTER TITLES: Several trophy/title plates inherited from Samurai Heroes do not correspond correctly to Utage's Japanese title order. Two cells are blank. The ending "Thank you for playing" tile is correctly English.
3. RESULTS / REWARDS: The current decorative English treasure/reward banner was flagged as not being separated into five independent game-defined strips. A proper silver separate-label candidate exists but is not yet approved for installation.
4. ROULETTE: Three luck result cards have English words but the visual style/typography needs further polishing.
5. STORY TITLES AND DUST CLOUD: Some cursive headings do not match the original Japanese brush-metallic style; a Dust Cloud/Open! image may appear cropped.
6. TROPHIES: The live signed PS3 trophy package is still the original Japanese. An English variant exists only as a separate unverified development candidate and is NOT in this beta.
7. SAVE IMPORT: Samurai Heroes USA/PAL save import is NOT supported. First/second boot, autosave and story/subtitles were marked PASS on the recent user's live build; clean-install behaviour on every emulator/hardware setup still needs confirmation.
8. PS3 HARDWARE/2P: Real console, local co-op and every game character/route have not received complete certification.

PLEASE DON'T REPORT THESE AS SURPRISES: They're already on the roadmap. Report a crash, missing dialogue or actual game freeze with the affected character/mode and the previous menu so the next patch can be targeted.
